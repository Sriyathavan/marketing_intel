# SPEC.md: Marketing Intelligence Engine, hackathon v1

Plan window: 90 days from `planStart` 2026-11-02 (Mon) to 2027-01-30 inclusive. Source of truth for all agents. `lib/types.ts` (already written by lead) is authoritative for types; if this doc and types.ts disagree, types.ts wins; ask the lead before changing it.

Never read or commit `.env.local` or `api key.md`.

## Conventions
- IDs: Trend `tr_<slug>`; Segment `seg_*`; Customer `c_0001`..`c_0200`; Opportunity `opp_<interest>__<segId>`; Sequence `seq_<oppId>`; Email `em_<seqId>_<n>`; Evidence `ev_<kind>_<ref>_<metric>`.
- Dates ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. Times `HH:mm` recipient-local.

## Data (Package A)
### customers.json
Generated by `scripts/generate-customers.ts`, seeded PRNG (mulberry32, seed 42), output committed. 200 rows. Distribution: lapsed 35, new 35, premium 30, family 40, deal 30, mindful 30 (each must land 20-50 under the classifier). Interest bias: premium snow_sports/eco_lodges/wildlife_safari; family family_adventure/camping/water_sports; deal hiking/camping/cycling; mindful eco_lodges/hiking/cycling; new mixed; lapsed mixed low engagement. 6% opted out. ~9% (18) carry one sensitive event, spread across segments. bestSendDay/Hour clustered per segment (family Sun 19:00, premium Tue 07:00, deal Thu 12:00). peakBookingMonth biased Nov-Feb for snow, Jan-Mar for summer trips.

### trends.json
12 keywords, 24 monthly points, ending 2026-09: hiking (`tr_hiking_trails_near_me`, `tr_backpacking_trips`), camping (`tr_winter_camping`, `tr_van_life_trips`), snow (`tr_ski_passes`, `tr_snowshoe_tours`), water (`tr_kayak_tours`), cycling (`tr_bikepacking`), wildlife (`tr_wildlife_safari`), family (`tr_family_adventure_trips`), eco (`tr_eco_lodges`, `tr_sustainable_travel`). yoyPct -5..+60, 5-6 clearly rising. Ski seasonality peaks Dec-Feb (~1.6); summer water/cycling Jun-Aug. `yoyPct` = round((mean last 12 / mean prior 12 - 1)*100), tested. Source label: "curated snapshot modelled on Google Trends (not live)".

### context.json
Per `ContextFile`. planEnd = planStart + 89 days. Holidays: Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Giving Tuesday, Hanukkah start, Christmas, NYE, New Year's, MLK weekend, resolution week. 4-5 launches, at least one `inventoryConstrained: true` (e.g. limited Patagonia departure). sendBlackouts 2026-12-25, 2027-01-01.

## Segmentation (`lib/segments.ts`), first match wins
1 `seg_lapsed` Lapsed Wanderers: engagementTrend dormant OR recencyDays>270
2 `seg_new` New Explorers: lifecycleStage in {subscriber_only, first_time_buyer}
3 `seg_premium` Premium Adventurers: ltv>=4000 OR tier gold/platinum
4 `seg_family` Family Getaways: composition in {couple_kids, single_parent, multigenerational}
5 `seg_deal` Deal Seekers: discountUsageRate>=0.5 OR buyingStyle deal_hunter
6 `seg_mindful` Mindful Explorers: everything else
Exports: `segmentOf`, `isSuppressed` (any SENSITIVE_EVENTS), eligible = emailOptIn && !suppressed, `buildSegmentProfiles(customers)` (stats over eligible except size/suppressedSensitive/optedOut), scoring-input builders.

### Candidates (`lib/opportunities.ts`, `buildOpportunities(): Opportunity[]`)
1. Candidate per (Interest x Segment); drop if no eligible members or no keyword.
2. Compute ScoringInput, call `scoreOpportunity`.
3. Sort total desc, trendMomentum desc, id asc.
4. Top 5, max 2 per segment and 2 per interest, greedy; rank 1..5.
5. offerType by segment: lapsed promo_discount; new content_story; premium early_access; family bundle; deal promo_discount; mindful new_trip_launch if launch of that interest exists else content_story.
6. Window = full 90 days.
7. 5-8 evidence items each, deterministic labels: yoy, 3-month slope, top rising query, affinity share, eligible count, click rate, season index, nearest holiday/launch, suppressed count.
8. title/thesis from string templates (no LLM).

## Scoring (`lib/scoring.ts`), pure and deterministic
`clamp01`, `round1`. Export `WEIGHTS`, `scoreOpportunity(input): ScoreBreakdown`.
| Factor | Weight | Score (0-100) |
|---|---|---|
| trendMomentum | 0.25 | 100*(0.6*clamp01(yoyPct/50)+0.4*clamp01(recentSlopePct/30)) |
| segmentFit | 0.25 | 100*(0.6*affinityShare+0.4*clamp01(meanClickRate/0.10)) |
| seasonalTiming | 0.20 | 100*(0.6*clamp01((avgSeasonIdx-0.7)/0.8)+0.4*bookingWindowShare) |
| revenuePotential | 0.20 | 100*clamp01(reachable*meanAov/120000) |
| effortRisk | 0.10 | 100 - clamp(0.5*effort+0.3*(100*fatigueShare)+0.2*(inventoryConstrained?100:0),0,100) |
Effort: content_story 15, early_access 30, promo_discount 40, bundle 45, new_trip_launch 55.
Inputs (eligible members): yoyPct = mean over keywords; recentSlopePct = mean of (last3/prior3 - 1)*100; affinityShare = share with interest in declared.interests OR clickByTopic>0 OR browsedInterests; meanClickRate; avgSeasonIdx = mean over keywords of seasonalityIndex averaged over 90 plan days by month; bookingWindowShare = share with peakBookingMonth in {11,12,1}; reachable = eligible with affinity; meanAov; fatigueShare from profile; inventoryConstrained = launch of same interest flagged.
Output: factor score round1; contribution round1(weight*score); total round1(sum(weight*raw)); explanation template string with key inputs; factors in rubric order.

## /api/plan (`app/api/plan/route.ts`, nodejs, POST) (Package B)
Request `PlanRequest`: a non-empty list of known opportunityIds else 400 `bad_request`.
Flow: load opps/profiles/context server-side; aggregates only to model. If no ANTHROPIC_API_KEY -> cache. Else `new Anthropic()`, model `claude-sonnet-5-5`, max_tokens 16000, tool `submit_plan` (tool_choice auto) (JSON schema from zod), 300s timeout. Payload: planStart/End, blackouts, holidays, launches, per opportunity: opportunity + segment profile (fatigueCapPerWeek, modalSendDay/Hour, dominantTone, suppressed count) + evidence with ids.
Schema `ModelPlan` (`lib/planSchema.ts`): sequences[] one per opportunity {opportunityId,name,goal,cadence,rationale,evidenceIds}, each 4-6 emails {step,sendDate,sendTimeLocal,subject<=70,preheader<=110,creativeDirection,cta{label,target},offerType,rationale,evidenceIds>=1}.
Post-process (`lib/guardrails.ts`): zod parse (retry once with errors, then cache); drop unknown evidenceIds (fallback to top evidence + warning); clamp sendDate into window, off blackouts (snap forward); enforce fatigueCapPerWeek emails/week/segment (move excess, warn); regex check; set suppressionNote; assign ids, status proposed, editedByUser false, source claude, model claude-sonnet-5-5.
Cache fallback: `data/cached-plan.json` (valid CampaignPlan for top-5), filter to requested ids (none match -> all with warning), source cache, model null, warning "Claude unavailable: showing cached plan". 500 `claude_failed` only if cache unreadable.

## Export (`lib/export.ts`, Package B)
`toCsv(plan, opps, segments)`: approved only; header exactly `sequence,opportunity,step,send_date,day,send_time_local,segment,audience_size,subject,preheader,creative_direction,cta_label,cta_target,offer_type,rationale,evidence_ids,edited`; RFC4180 quoting, CRLF, evidence ids joined `;`, prefix leading `= + - @` with `'`.
`toMarkdown(...)`: approved only; H1 + window; section per opportunity with score total + factor table; weekly calendar table (Week, Date, Sequence, Subject); per-email details/rationale/evidence labels; footer with suppression notes and statement that sensitive signals are used for suppression only.

## Guardrails
G1 sensitive-event (divorce, bereavement, health_journey) customers removed from audiences/eligible/reach/affinity; suppression only. G2 model sees aggregates only. G3 system prompt forbids referencing life events, health, grief, relationship status, weight, age, gender in copy. G4 regex `divorce|widow|bereave|grief|passed away|diagnos|weight loss|recover(y|ing) from|fresh start after|single again|empty nest|your age` over subject/preheader/creativeDirection/rationale -> `copy_sensitive_term:<term>` flag. G5 server-written suppressionNote per sequence ("N of M segment members (x%) excluded for sensitive life events; used for suppression only"). G6 opt-outs excluded. G7 weekly cap by fatigue; lapsed max 1/week. G8 no gender targeting.

API: mocked valid response -> source claude; invalid -> retry -> cache; API error -> cache+warning; missing key -> cache; bad/empty ids -> 400; unknown evidence stripped; dates clamped; weekly cap; regex flags; suppressionNote present; cached-plan validates.
