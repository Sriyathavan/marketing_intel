Marketing Intelligence Engine — Project Plan

3. What the prototype will include
Inputs: Load synthetic customer segments, search signals and seasonal signals.
Opportunity discovery: Produce 3–5 relevant opportunities with supporting evidence.
Transparent scoring: Calculate priority scores using an agreed rubric.
Campaign planning: Generate a structured 90-day plan.
Review: Allow users to edit, approve or reject recommendations.
Export: Download the plan as CSV or Markdown.

1. Project objective
Build a working prototype that turns customer, search and seasonal signals into 3–5 prioritized marketing opportunities and an editable 90-day campaign plan.
The prototype must show how recommendations connect to evidence, how priorities are calculated, and where a marketer reviews and approves the plan.
Initial scope: Outdoor/adventure travel, synthetic customer data, curated external signal datasets and plans generated on demand.
Primary user: A marketing strategist responsible for campaign planning. This is a working assumption to validate during preparation.
